# Career Relic Catalogue

Each relic keeps its original signature property and two additional bespoke intrinsic properties. The former fourth bespoke property is replaced by one real native TOR enchantment, blessing or Dawi rune selected for the career and relic item type. Cumulative set-tier bonuses are documented separately in `SETS.md`.

- **Foresight of Azyr:** 15% extra lightning damage and 15% physical resistance; 15% chance to grant nearby troops the same boons for 20 seconds upon dealing damage. *(native TOR enchantment/blessing/rune)*
